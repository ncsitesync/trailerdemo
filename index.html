How a Move Works

Four steps. No surprises on move day.

You should know what happens next at every point. Here it is.

  1. Free On-Site Estimate

    We walk the home and both lots, measure the route, and give you a written price. Nothing to sign yet.

  2. Permits & Route Planning

    Our office pulls the NCDOT permits and maps a path around low wires, tight turns, and weight-limited bridges.

  3. Transport Day

    Licensed drivers, pilot cars front and rear. You get a text when we leave and another when we arrive.

  4. Setup & Final Inspection

    We block, level, anchor, and skirt it, then walk you through a final level check before we leave.
